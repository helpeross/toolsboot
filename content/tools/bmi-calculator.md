The BMI Calculator computes your Body Mass Index from height and weight, classifies it using the WHO categories (underweight, normal, overweight, obesity classes), and shows a color-coded scale with your position marked on it.

It also displays the ideal weight range for a normal BMI given your height, plus the midpoint. Sliders and number inputs stay in sync, and metric/imperial units are supported. Calculation is instant and stays in your browser.
