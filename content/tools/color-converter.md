The Color Converter translates colors between HEX, RGB and HSL the moment you type. Enter any format on the left - or pick a color with the eyedropper - and the other formats, the CSS declarations and a preview swatch update instantly on the right.

The converted values are formatted ready to paste into CSS: hex, rgb() and hsl() syntax are all shown. Everything runs locally in your browser, so colors can be converted even from completely offline design work.
