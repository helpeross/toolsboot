Compare two versions of any text entirely in your browser:

- Paste or type **Input A** and **Input B**, then hit **Compare** (or keep **Live** on for instant updates as you type).
- The result is shown side by side: unchanged lines in grey, **removed** lines in red on the left, **added** lines in green on the right. Line numbers make it easy to locate changes.
- Both panes scroll in sync; the status bar counts identical, removed and added lines.
- Copy the diff as a `-` / `+` text summary, or **Clear** to reset.

The diff uses a line-level LCS algorithm, so even large documents compare quickly and accurately. Everything runs locally - your text never leaves your device.
