Generate QR codes entirely in your browser:

- Type or paste any text, URL, Wi-Fi config or other content - the QR code renders instantly (Live is on by default).
- **Size** slider (120-600 px), **Margin** (quiet zone, 0-10 modules), **Error correction** L / M / Q / H (higher level = more damage resistance, up to ~30%).
- Pick custom **foreground and background colors** - great for brand-matching codes.
- **Download PNG** saves the generated code at full pixel size.

Everything runs locally - your content never leaves your device. The QR encoder is a bundled open-source library (qrcode-generator, MIT).
