The Tip Calculator works out the tip and total for any bill. Pick a quick preset (10-25%) or drag the slider for a custom percentage, set how many people are splitting the bill, and see the per-person share at a glance.

An optional setting rounds each person's share up to the nearest dollar, which makes settling cash payments easier. The four result cards update live. Everything is computed locally in your browser.
