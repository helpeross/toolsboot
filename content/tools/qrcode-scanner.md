Decode QR codes from images entirely in your browser:

- **Click to select** one or more images, **drag & drop** them, or **paste** (Ctrl+V) a screenshot - JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF and WEBP supported.
- Each image is decoded locally - nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Results show the decoded value with a copy button per item, plus **Copy All**.
- The status bar tracks how many images were decoded and how many failed.
- Large images are automatically downscaled when needed for more reliable detection.

The decoder is a bundled open-source library (jsQR) - works fully offline.
