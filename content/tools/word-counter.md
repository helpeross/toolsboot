The Word Counter tracks words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and paragraphs in real time as you type or paste. It also estimates reading time based on an average of 200 words per minute.

The stats update instantly, making it useful for essays, social posts, articles and any text with a length requirement. A copy button exports the whole stats block. Counting is done locally in your browser - your text never leaves the device.
