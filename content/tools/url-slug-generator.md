The URL Slug Generator turns any title or phrase into a clean, SEO-friendly slug. It transliterates common accented characters, lowercases, strips punctuation and joins words with hyphens or underscores. Options let you disable lowercase, keep trailing separators, or switch the separator.

A good slug matches the page topic in a readable way, which helps search engines and users alike. Paste a headline and copy the result straight into your URL. Conversion runs entirely in your browser.
