The Email Extractor scans any pasted text and pulls out every valid-looking email address, one per line. The dedupe option removes repeats, and the counter shows how many addresses were found.

This is handy for cleaning up contact lists, log files and exported data. The extraction runs with a standard email pattern entirely in your browser - your text is never uploaded, so it is safe for private contact data.
