The Image Compressor shrinks JPG, PNG and WEBP images right in your browser. Drag an image in or click to choose one, then tune quality, max width and output format; a live preview shows the compressed result alongside original size, new size and the percentage saved. The Download button saves the optimized image with one click.

Compression is done with the Canvas API entirely on your device - your photos never get uploaded, which keeps even sensitive images private.
