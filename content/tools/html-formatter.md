The HTML Formatter pretty-prints or minifies HTML in one click. Paste markup on the left and it is re-indented with proper nesting on the right, while the contents of <script>, <style>, <pre> and <!-- comments --> are left untouched so your code never gets mangled. A Minify mode strips whitespace between tags for the smallest payload.

Formatting is done with a lightweight parser, not by loading your HTML into a DOM, so no external resources are fetched and nothing executes. Every operation runs locally in your browser - your markup never leaves the device.
